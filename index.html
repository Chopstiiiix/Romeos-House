The House

An intimate cultural lounge in Lagos — a curated community of tastemakers and creative entrepreneurs who value meaningful connection, privacy and refined social experience.

Romeo's House is members-led and built around intentional cultural programming: a premium cocktail offering, music showcases, DJ sessions and quiet creative gatherings, all in a dimly lit, vintage-chic setting. Come for the room; stay for the people in it.
